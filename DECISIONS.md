# Design Decisions — AURA Premium Home Page

## 1. Why this design approach?

I chose a premium dark SaaS visual direction for AURA because the product is positioned as an AI-powered automation platform. I wanted the first screen to communicate the product value immediately through a strong headline, clear CTA, and a realistic product dashboard rather than relying on generic marketing copy.

I considered a more traditional light SaaS layout, but chose the dark interface because it creates stronger visual contrast with the purple and cyan accent colors and makes the product dashboard feel more focused and premium.

## 2. Trade-off made under the time limit

Because of the limited time, I focused on making the home page polished and responsive instead of building backend functionality or real authentication. I prioritized the hero section, product visualization, responsive layouts, and a small number of meaningful interactions.

With a full week, I would add more detailed product interactions, stronger accessibility testing, performance optimization, and additional visual states for the dashboard.

## 3. AI usage and personal verification

I used AI tools to help generate and structure parts of the HTML, CSS, and JavaScript implementation. I personally reviewed the generated code, adjusted the visual styling, checked the responsive layout, verified the navigation and interactions, and tested the deployed page.

The final implementation uses vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript so that it can be understood and maintained without a framework.
